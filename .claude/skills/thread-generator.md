# Thread Generator

Create Twitter threads for Monday 9am posting.

## Thread Structure

10-15 tweets following this arc:

### Tweet 1: The Hook
- Create strong curiosity
- Promise specific value
- Patterns:
  - "Here's why [mainstream belief] is wrong:"
  - "I analyzed 50 [X] and found [surprising insight]"
  - "This [example] did [result] by doing [counterintuitive thing]"
  - "Everyone's doing [X]. Here's why you shouldn't:"

### Tweets 2-3: The Setup
- Establish the problem
- Show stakes
- Build tension

### Tweets 4-10: The Breakdown
- 5-7 key insights
- Each tweet = one clear point
- Use specific examples
- Include numbers and data

### Tweets 11-13: The Transformation
- Before → After
- How to apply this
- The mechanism

### Tweet 14: The Lesson
- Main takeaway
- Quotable insight
- Ties back to hook

### Tweet 15: The CTA
- Soft call to action
- "If this helped, follow for more"
- Or point to WBYO

## Hook Templates

**Promise a breakdown:**
"I've audited 50+ offers this year. Here's what separates the ones that close from the ones that don't:"

**Shock value:**
"47 case studies. $500k in ad spend. 15% close rate. Here's what was wrong:"

**Case study:**
"This agency doubled their close rate in 6 weeks. They didn't get better at sales. They fixed their offer mechanics:"

**Contrarian:**
"Everyone's telling you to get more testimonials. I'm telling you to stop showing them on sales calls. Here's why:"

**Personal:**
"I made this mistake for 2 years. Cost me easily $200k in revenue. Here's what I learned:"

## Voice Requirements

- Each tweet under 280 chars
- No hashtags
- Numbered format: "1/" through "15/"
- Direct, punchy tone
- Specific examples throughout
- End with bang, not whimper

## Output

Generate 7 thread hook options, then expand the best one into a full 10-15 tweet thread.
