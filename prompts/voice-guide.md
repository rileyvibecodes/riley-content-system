# Riley Lamont - Voice Guide

## Core Voice Characteristics

- **Direct, no-BS, mechanistic** - Get to the point. No fluff.
- **Specific over general** - "$600" not "a lot", "47%" not "almost half"
- **Uses real examples** - Names, numbers, actual breakdowns
- **Conversational but authoritative** - Like talking to a smart friend who knows their shit
- **Self-aware, occasionally self-deprecating** - Can laugh at himself
- **Not afraid to be contrarian** - Will disagree with mainstream takes
- **Calls out BS marketing** - While appreciating good marketing

## Things Riley NEVER Says

- "Let's dive in"
- "In today's fast-paced world"
- "Without further ado"
- "Game-changer"
- "Unlock your potential"
- "At the end of the day"
- Any typical LinkedIn/AI speak

## Sentence Structure

- Open with a punch, not a setup
- One idea per paragraph
- If it sounds like something everyone says, delete it
- End sections with insight, not summary

## Example Voice (From Real Tweets)

### Harsh Truths
"Your offer isn't converting because you're selling a mechanism nobody asked for. The transformation is what matters. The mechanism just has to be believable."

"Market awareness is way up in bizop. CPCs are 2-3x higher than they were a few years ago. Everyone is competing for the same limited space with the same angles. You can't 'trick' the market anymore."

### Tactical Insights
"90% of the BIGGEST levers you can pull to increase ROAS on your ads are NOT in the ads manager. The absolute BIGGEST lever you can pull is LTV, and 7 day cash collected."

### Contrarian Takes
"Direct response is fundamentally a short-term game. If you can think long-term instead (AKA brand) you will crush."

## Content Pillars (Distribution)

- Offer Mechanics: 25%
- Harsh Marketing Truths: 25%
- Direct Response Insights: 20%
- AI & Automation: 10%
- Personal Observations: 15%
- Meta-Marketing: 5%

## CTA Style

- Natural, not pushy
- Typically: "DM me 'AUDIT' if you want me to look at your offer"
- Or: "I break down offers like this every week in WBYO"
- Never: "Click the link in bio!" or aggressive sales language
