# Content Calendar Generator

Build Riley's weekly content calendar.

## Weekly Structure

### Monday
- **9:00 AM**: THREAD (10-15 tweets, biggest idea of the week)
- **12:00 PM**: Tweet (marketing insight or breakdown)
- **3:00 PM**: Tweet (actionable tip)
- **LinkedIn**: Optional (Tue/Thu are primary days)

### Tuesday
- **9:00 AM**: Tweet (harsh truth or big idea)
- **12:00 PM**: Tweet (offer mechanics insight)
- **3:00 PM**: Tweet (one-liner or quick tip)
- **LinkedIn 8:00 AM**: Main LinkedIn post

### Wednesday
- **9:00 AM**: Tweet (counterintuitive insight)
- **12:00 PM**: Tweet (marketing breakdown)
- **3:00 PM**: Tweet (actionable tip)

### Thursday
- **9:00 AM**: Tweet (harsh truth)
- **12:00 PM**: Tweet (offer mechanics)
- **3:00 PM**: Tweet (observation or one-liner)
- **LinkedIn 12:00 PM**: Second LinkedIn post

### Friday
- **9:00 AM**: Tweet (big idea or contrarian)
- **12:00 PM**: Tweet (tactical insight)
- **3:00 PM**: Tweet (one-liner)

### Saturday (Optional)
- **10:00 AM**: Tweet (personal observation)
- **2:00 PM**: Tweet (lighter content)
- **LinkedIn 10:00 AM**: Optional third post

### Sunday
- **10:00 AM**: Tweet (reflection or insight)
- **2:00 PM**: Tweet (prepare for Monday thread)
- **5:00 PM**: Tweet (week preview)

## Time Slot Strategy

### Morning (9am)
Best for:
- Big ideas
- Threads
- Harsh truths
- Contrarian takes
- Thought-provoking content

### Midday (12pm)
Best for:
- Marketing insights
- Tactical breakdowns
- Case studies
- How-to content

### Afternoon (3pm)
Best for:
- Actionable tips
- One-liners
- Quick insights
- Engagement bait

## Content Pillar Balance

Weekly distribution (21 tweets):
- **Offer Mechanics**: 5-6 tweets (25%)
- **Harsh Marketing Truths**: 5-6 tweets (25%)
- **Direct Response Insights**: 4-5 tweets (20%)
- **Personal Observations**: 3-4 tweets (15%)
- **AI & Automation**: 2-3 tweets (10%)
- **Meta-Marketing**: 1-2 tweets (5%)

## Calendar Output Format

```markdown
# Week of [Date] - Content Calendar

## Monday
| Time | Platform | Type | Content | Pillar |
|------|----------|------|---------|--------|
| 9:00 AM | Twitter | Thread | [Hook first tweet] | [Pillar] |
| 12:00 PM | Twitter | Tweet | [Full text] | [Pillar] |
| 3:00 PM | Twitter | Tweet | [Full text] | [Pillar] |

[Continue for each day]

## Weekly Stats
- Total Tweets: 21
- Thread: 1 (Monday)
- LinkedIn Posts: 2-3
- Pillar Breakdown: [percentages]

## Predicted Top Performers
1. [Tweet] - Why: [reason]
2. [Tweet] - Why: [reason]
3. [Tweet] - Why: [reason]

## Engagement Reminders
- Reply to comments within 30 mins of posting
- First hour is critical for engagement
- Quote tweet interesting responses
```

## Variety Rules

- No same pillar back-to-back in a day
- No similar tweet formats in same time slot across days
- Monday thread topic should NOT appear in other tweets
- Mix harsh truths with actionable content
- End each day with something punchy (3pm slot)
