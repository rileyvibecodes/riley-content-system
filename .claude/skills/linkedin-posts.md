# LinkedIn Post Generator

Create LinkedIn posts adapted for professional audience.

## LinkedIn vs Twitter

| Aspect | Twitter | LinkedIn |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Tone | Most direct, punchy | Professional but still direct |
| Length | Under 280 chars | 400-600 words |
| Depth | One insight | Full tactical breakdown |
| CTA | Soft or none | Professional DM invite |
| Format | Dense paragraphs ok | Heavy line breaks |

## Post Structure

### Line 1-2: The Hook
- Must stop the scroll
- No fluff, straight to value
- Examples:
  - "Your close rate isn't a sales problem."
  - "I just finished auditing an offer with 47 case studies. Their close rate? 15%."

### Lines 3-10: The Setup
- Establish problem or observation
- Use specific example
- Short paragraphs (1-2 sentences)
- Heavy line breaks for readability

### Lines 11-25: The Breakdown
- 3-5 key insights
- Each with tactical depth
- Include frameworks if relevant
- Specific numbers and examples

### Lines 26-30: The Lesson
- Main takeaway
- Quotable insight
- Business outcome focus

### Lines 31-35: The CTA
- Professional invitation
- Options:
  - "DM me 'AUDIT' if you want me to look at your offer"
  - "Drop a comment if this resonates"
  - "Follow for more offer mechanics"

## Formatting Rules

- Line breaks after every 1-2 sentences
- Short paragraphs only
- Use bold sparingly for emphasis
- No hashtags (or max 3 at end)
- No emojis except strategic placement

## Topics That Work

1. **Case study breakdowns** - Real examples with numbers
2. **Framework shares** - Tactical how-tos
3. **Contrarian takes** - Challenge industry norms
4. **Lessons learned** - Personal experience stories
5. **Offer mechanics deep dives** - Technical insights

## Voice Adaptation

Still Riley's voice but slightly:
- More professional context
- Deeper tactical explanation
- Business outcome focused
- Longer-form storytelling

Avoid:
- "I'm excited to share..."
- "I'm humbled by..."
- Any corporate LinkedIn speak

## Output

Generate 3 LinkedIn posts:
1. One case study/breakdown
2. One tactical framework
3. One contrarian/observation piece

Each with full text ready to post.
